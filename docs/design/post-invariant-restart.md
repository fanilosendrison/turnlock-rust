# Post-invariant restart

The restart point is TURNLOCK's current Product Intent, canonical terminology,
stable invariants, and preserved open boundaries. Their preservation does not
establish whole-product completeness, consistency, realizability, or
implementation conformance.

The next investigation must determine what question to establish, for which
scope, under which premises, through which examinations, and with which limits
on admissible conclusions and evidence. It must distinguish necessary
obligations from unresolved product choices and selected mechanisms.

No replacement assurance method, formalism, model, review campaign, runner, or
runtime implementation has been selected. Archives and external research may be
reexamined only as information within their actual provenance and scope; they
are not inherited constraints or TURNLOCK authority.

The separately scoped investigation is tracked by
[Issue #93](https://github.com/fanilosendrison/turnlock-rust/issues/93).
