# Competitive Guarantee Watch Methodology

Methodology version: 1.0.0

This version identifies the research method, not a TURNLOCK product version. The
method governs competitive-watch operations only. Its axes are research
questions grounded in existing TURNLOCK authorities; they are not new TURNLOCK
invariants, obligations, architecture, or derivation premises.

## Guarantee comparison

The following axes guide evidence collection and scoped assessment.

### T1 — Workflow-owned orchestration

Determine whether executable workflow semantics own declared global
orchestration and permitted continuations while the runtime executes them,
without hidden agent scheduling or runtime-invented strategy.

### T2 — Distinct execution forms / minimum-sufficient cognition

Determine whether mechanical execution, bounded raw LLM inference, bounded
independent-agent execution, and main-agent continuation remain semantically
distinct and can be selected according to the minimum-sufficient-cognition
principle.

### T3 — Same-main continuation / cognitive lineage

Determine whether a declared main-agent continuation preserves the existing
interactive main-agent cognitive lineage and ordinary agency rather than
silently substituting a fresh, reconstructed, or one-shot execution resource.

### T4 — Structured invocation, nesting, recursion and exact immediate-caller return

Determine whether workflow invocation preserves structured caller contexts,
supports nested and recursive/cyclic occurrences as distinct invocations, and
returns normal completion to the exact immediate caller.

### T5 — Invocation authority / composition admissibility / transitive restrictions

Determine whether invocation authority and composition admissibility remain
separate, applicable restrictions survive nested and recursive composition, and
unknown required compatibility fails closed before acceptance.

### T6 — Concurrency, causal ordering, joins and conditional progression

Determine whether declared concurrency preserves branch-local semantics,
causal ordering, main-lineage non-forkability, join requirements, and eventual
advance of each continuously eligible and applicable TURNLOCK-owned progression
occurrence.

### T7 — Governing-definition binding per accepted invocation

Determine whether each accepted invocation binds exactly one governing workflow
definition no later than acceptance, keeps it stable for that invocation, and
uses the definition admitted for the same acceptance decision.

### T8 — Realized execution / realized-prefix truth and unknown terminal facts

Determine whether inspectable execution truth faithfully preserves the realized
execution or realized prefix, occurrence and causal distinctions where needed,
and only terminal facts actually known without inventing unrealized work or
unknown causes.

### T9 — Effective-condition provenance and protected-known-vs-unknown distinction

Determine whether TURNLOCK-known effective conditions remain attributable to
the scopes they governed through a realizable capture handoff, while protected
known conditions remain distinct from unknown or unavailable conditions.

### T10 — Harness independence / runtime-composable external realizations

Determine whether semantics remain harness-independent and semantically
preserving external realizations can be established through a supported runtime
composition path when the accepted trigger applies, without silent bypass.

### C1 — Managed execution / isolation / workspace / effects / elasticity

Determine which managed environment lifecycle, isolation, workspace
materialization, result/effect extraction, resource allocation, and elastic
execution responsibilities are actually supplied and under which boundaries.

### C2 — Durable run signal and observation-time capture

Determine what execution truth and condition provenance are actually captured
at observation time, retained durably, attributable to the correct scopes, and
unavailable for later reconstruction if omitted.

### C3 — Layered reproducibility profiles

Determine which declared layer is supported: governing-method identity,
condition comparability, environment rematerialization, control replay, or
fresh output reproduction, including every assumption and unsupported layer.

### C4 — Property-relative comparison eligibility

Determine whether an evaluation contract identifies property-relevant
determinants and establishes equality, controlled difference, paired or blocked
treatment, modeled variation, or explicit unknown status before comparison.

### C5 — Evaluation and regression validity / attribution

Determine whether observed differences are distinguished from justified
method-level conclusions and whether method, execution path, realization,
environment, input, evaluator, result, efficiency, and unexplained variation
are separated where evidence permits.

### C6 — Variance control and experimental efficiency

Determine whether paired, blocked, controlled, stratified, normalized, or other
justified designs reduce residual unexplained variance and improve information
per execution without claiming deterministic outputs.

### C7 — Optimization/refinement/promotion signal validity

Determine whether workflow generation, refinement, selection, and promotion
consume an explicitly qualified comparison signal, preserve uncertainty and
confounding, and avoid treating a higher score under changed conditions as
method improvement.

### C8 — Longitudinal downstream uses

Examine routing, benchmarks, datasets, training signal, learning, research,
assurance, incident analysis, and other longitudinal uses. For every use,
identify the required signal, its producer, missing dependencies, authorization,
and actual availability.

These questions never require an alternative to adopt an architecture that
TURNLOCK has not itself adopted. Equivalent available compositions are assessed
by their actual guarantees and boundaries, not by resemblance to TURNLOCK names
or assumed mechanisms.

## Permanent radar

Once an identifiable actor, product, or component is evaluated on any scope, it
remains monitored indefinitely while the watch operates unless the user gives
an explicit instruction to stop monitoring it.

An actor is never removed automatically because of:

- a low threat level;
- a complementary role;
- a lack of news;
- an indeterminate assessment;
- closed source;
- an archived repository;
- closure of a finding; or
- acquisition, renaming, or cessation of activity.

Monitoring membership, actor lifecycle, relevance, capability, and confidence
are separate facts. A dormant or discontinued actor retains its history and is
checked for revival, successors, forks, and transferred technology.

A rename preserves actor identity. A genuine fork or successor receives a
linked identity. An identity error is corrected in a new report without erasing
history.

The effective historical radar is the union of:

- initial seeds;
- admissions in every prior report;
- explicit user additions; and
- pending registrations recovered with verifiable references.

`watchlist.json` does not override or replace admissions recorded in reports. A
user instruction to stop monitoring changes monitoring status while preserving
the actor and all reports.

## Two mandatory activities for every execution

Every execution performs both activities:

- **A — accumulated-radar monitoring:** inspect every retained actor according
  to current coverage obligations and cursors;
- **B — open discovery:** search for new actors, products, components, forks,
  research projects, features, and available compositions.

Neither activity may be omitted because the other consumed available resources.
When either cannot be completed, the report declares incomplete coverage.
Discovery occurs on every run, not only on Monday, and is not limited to known
names.

Search by problems and guarantees, rotating formulations across:

- same-main coding-agent continuation inside executable workflows;
- structured nested workflow invocation and immediate-caller return;
- workflow governing-definition binding and live-edit isolation;
- composition admissibility and transitive restrictions;
- durable workflow execution and replay boundaries;
- execution-condition provenance and scoped condition identity;
- managed isolated execution and external-effect handling;
- layered reproducibility and property-relative comparability;
- regression attribution and drift separation;
- controlled or variance-reducing agent evaluation;
- workflow optimization and promotion under uncertainty; and
- available end-to-end compositions across harness, runtime, sandbox,
  evaluation, and optimizer components.

Include new products, research projects, new features from known actors, forks,
and compositions that are actually available. Record the channels, queries,
and times actually used. “No new entrant found” is acceptable only after a real
search and never proves that no entrant exists. A hypothetical capability that
would still need to be developed is not an available composition.

## Implementation analysis

Do not stop at public positioning when a conclusion depends on code behavior.
For each relevant guarantee, follow this path when accessible:

```text
production entry point → workflow/session control → binding/admission →
persistence/capture → error/unknown handling → execution/result attribution →
comparison/evaluation → optimizer/promotion when applicable
```

Inspect:

- configurations and defaults;
- features actually reachable in the product;
- disableable controls;
- error and bypass paths;
- which actor may change workflow, evaluation, or promotion policy;
- wrappers and delegated components;
- positive and negative tests; and
- differences among branches, releases, and deployed services.

Monitor relevant source changes even without a public announcement. Reproduce
useful tests or counterexamples when the environment permits safe, isolated
execution without user secrets.

Never collapse these evidence states:

```text
code read
!= tests read
!= tests executed
!= verified proof
```

When code is closed, inaccessible, or incomplete, preserve the limitation,
continue monitoring, inspect available primary material, and do not infer the
absence of a capability. An unfinished technical inspection becomes a
persistent investigation. A component whose identity is still indeterminate is
represented explicitly as indeterminate rather than assigned an invented
identity.

## Discriminating scenarios

Retain exactly these scenarios as analysis questions:

1. same-main repeated workflow/main handoffs;
2. nested workflow from main region returns to exact caller region;
3. D1 active invocation while artifact becomes D2, later invocation may bind D2;
4. heterogeneous fan-out with branch-local main handoff;
5. two unordered concurrent uses of same main lineage;
6. independent-agent-selected nested subtree attempting main-lineage reachability;
7. admission/binding race where validated and governing definitions could diverge;
8. accepted external realization silently bypassed or unavailable;
9. protected known condition compared across runs without collapsing to generic unknown;
10. partial realized prefix and unknown terminal cause;
11. occurrence-scoped conditional non-starvation;
12. disposable compute destruction with ambiguous external effect;
13. W17/W18 score difference with missing property-required determinant;
14. unchanged method with model/tool/environment realization drift;
15. optimizer selects W18 under changed evaluator/dataset/model conditions;
16. W2 authored while W1 remains active;
17. durable control replay mistaken for fresh semantic-output reproducibility;
18. regression dashboard with stable dataset but missing provider/harness/environment provenance.

These scenarios do not constitute a normative TURNLOCK benchmark installed by
this work.

## Levels and dimensions

Assess these dimensions separately:

- `turnlock_core_guarantees`;
- `managed_execution`;
- `run_signal_reproducibility`; and
- `evaluation_optimization_downstream`.

No aggregate score or average is required.

| Level | Rendered label | Meaning |
| ----- | -------------- | ------- |
| `U` | ⚪ INDETERMINATE | Information is insufficient; this does not mean low threat. |
| `L0` | 🟢 ADJACENT_OR_COMPLEMENTARY | The examined scope does not replace the considered guarantee. |
| `L1` | 🟡 ISOLATED_PRIMITIVE | A relevant primitive exists without the required composed guarantee. |
| `L2` | 🟠 SUBSTANTIAL_PARTIAL_SUBSTITUTE | A substantial subset is established and identified gaps remain. |
| `L3` | 🔴 NEAR_EQUIVALENCE | A composition covers most of the declared scope and decisive gaps are explicit. |
| `L4` | 🟣 DEMONSTRATED_SCOPED_EQUIVALENCE | End-to-end equivalence is established for the declared scope with sufficient assumptions, dependencies, and verification. |

Every rating states its scope, rationale, evidence, confidence, and external
dependencies. Violet on a sub-scope is not equivalence to all of Turnlock Cloud.
A downstream platform may rely on an external signal producer.

Availability, role, confidence, and lifecycle remain separate. Reputation,
funding, and repository stars do not change technical levels. An announcement
may trigger investigation but cannot promote an established level. No color
removes an actor from the radar.

## Evidence discipline

Prefer primary sources. Source-code evidence identifies immutable revisions and
precise paths. Distinguish event date, publication date, and observation date.

Evidence categories are:

- `claim`;
- `documentation`;
- `source_inspection`;
- `tests_inspected`;
- `reproduced`; and
- `proof_artifact`.

For `reproduced` evidence, preserve the command, environment, result, and a
reference to execution material actually obtained. A formal-proof artifact is
described according to the scope it actually proves; its filename or extension
never establishes proof status automatically. Event and publication dates may
retain limited precision rather than inventing an unsupported time. Never turn
an old chat answer into a verified audit. Never invent a SHA, URL, test, result,
or author.

Preserve these distinctions:

- workflow durability is not fresh-output reproducibility;
- a trace is not comparison eligibility;
- a score difference is not method attribution;
- missing provenance is not equality evidence;
- capture is not retention;
- environment rematerialization is not universal determinant control;
- first observed regression is not proven exact cause;
- absence of public evidence is not impossibility;
- announcement is not availability; and
- a useful synthetic example is not an original historical observation.

Apply the same standard to TURNLOCK: intended behavior is not an implemented or
verified guarantee, and a possible Cloud opportunity is not an available
product.

## History and trajectories

Every published report is immutable. A correction is a new report that
references the original. Actor, component, and finding identifiers remain
stable.

Classify assessment changes independently with:

- `implementation_change`;
- `new_evidence`;
- `assessment_correction`;
- `turnlock_baseline_change`; and
- `methodology_change`.

Only `implementation_change` establishes technical progress by the alternative.
Do not rewrite historical ratings under a new methodology or TURNLOCK baseline.
Each report preserves the TURNLOCK commit, schema version, and methodology
version used. Method and schema sources must be recoverable at immutable
revisions.

A carried-forward assessment retains its original observation date and sets
`revalidated_this_run` to `false`. Never derive an equivalence date from commit
counts. Analyze gaps actually closed, reduced, reopened, or made dependent on
other systems.

## Publication controls beyond JSON Schema

The structural schema is necessary but insufficient. Before publication, the
execution must also verify:

- uniqueness of identifiers and actor/component pairs;
- resolution of evidence and historical finding references;
- presence of every axis, using explicit `unknown` when not evaluated;
- consistency among `report_id`, `generated_at`, and the report directory;
- temporal consistency of the observation window;
- consistency of `new_entrants`, `admitted_actor_ids`, and
  `newly_admitted_actor_ids`;
- equality between `current_actor_ids` and the set of actor IDs in `actors`;
- inclusion of all historical actors and every new admission;
- `user_stopped` only with a verifiable user instruction;
- no fictitious revalidation or cursor advancement without observation;
- justification for every threat level;
- distinction between a carried-forward value and a new observation;
- JSON/Markdown equivalence; and
- immutability of every previously published report.

Successful structural validation never establishes the truth of competitive
conclusions or inter-report continuity. Those require evidence review and the
separate continuity checks above.
